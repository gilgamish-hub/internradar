How it works

  1. You enroll. Field, skills you already have, cities you can work in, the lowest stipend worth your time, and when you want your digest.
  2. The portal scans. Every few hours it pulls fresh internship postings for the fields its students care about.
  3. Each posting is scored out of 100 against your profile: title, how many of your field's skills appear in the description, the stipend, the city.
  4. At your time, your digest arrives with the top matches and a line on why each one matched. Nothing repeats — once sent, it never comes back.

What it refuses to send you

Any posting that mentions a registration fee, a security deposit or a “guaranteed placement” package is dropped before it ever reaches a student. Course-style “internships” — the ones that sell you a certificate — are allowed through but marked, so you can see what you are looking at. A real employer never asks a candidate for money.

You stay in control

Change your field, cities, stipend floor or delivery time whenever you like — the next digest follows the new settings. Pause deliveries during exams. Delete your account and every row that belongs to it goes with it.
