What it refuses to send

Any posting mentioning a registration fee, a security deposit or a “guaranteed placement” package is dropped before it reaches a student. Course-style “internships” — the ones selling a certificate — are allowed through but clearly marked, so you can see what you are looking at. A real employer never asks a candidate for money.

You stay in control

Change your field, cities, stipend floor or delivery time whenever you like — the next digest follows the new settings. Pause during exams. Delete your account and every row belonging to it goes with it.
